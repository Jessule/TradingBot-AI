How to play

Drag a dirty item from the belt into the washer. When the light turns green, drag the clean load to the orange tube for 1 coin. The counter on the washer shows how many items are inside; with a bigger tub, add more and press the green button to start (a full washer starts on its own).

You can drop clothes on the floor, but they get filthy and crumble into sand, and sand slows the belt. To clean it: put the dustpan next to the sand, push the sand into it with the broom, then empty the dustpan in the trash can.

Deliver loads back to back for a combo (up to x2), and watch for golden clothes: they pay x10. Earn 500 coins in a run to unlock Golden Hangers (prestige).

Tapping works too: tap something, then tap where it goes.
